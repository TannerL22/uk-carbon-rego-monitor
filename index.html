How to read this dashboard

This dashboard tests whether representative customer renewable electricity claims are backed by eligible REGO evidence. It estimates uncovered MWh and assumed cover cost, then adds FMD, grid-emissions, and carbon-market context. It does not calculate official customer Scope 2 emissions or provide live trading data.

What requires attention

    Customer Renewable Claim Coverage

    This section tests whether representative customer/product renewable electricity claims are backed by eligible REGO evidence. FMD, grid, and carbon-market data are context only and do not determine claim validity.

    Claim Evidence Register

    Operational audit trail linking customer claims, certificate exceptions, source evidence, remediation ownership, and customer/FMD impact.

    REGO Contract Reconciliation

    Representative demo supplier-style REGO ledger reconciled against contract eligibility, delivery periods, and assumed replacement prices.

    Exception Register

    The control evidence: duplicate IDs, lifecycle errors, contract mismatches, vintage breaches, and source-quality issues.

    FMD & Emissions-Reporting Context

    GOV.UK Fuel Mix Disclosure factors provide disclosure-period context for contracted and uncovered MWh. They do not validate renewable claims or calculate official customer Scope 2 emissions.

    Carbon-Cost Market Context

    Indicative UKA carbon-cost context converts selected emissions factors into GBP/MWh. It is not a bill calculation, power-price forecast, or REGO claim validation input.

    Future Scope 2 Readiness

    Consultation-aware data-readiness view for future hourly matching and deliverability expectations. This does not apply final Scope 2 rules, calculate official customer emissions, or change current annual REGO claim status.

    GB Power Fundamentals

    Recent NESO Carbon Intensity API data fetched during the Python build. Physical emissions context is separate from contractual renewable supply.
